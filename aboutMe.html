My Story

Dedicated to catching functional bugs and perfecting user experiences.

My name is Janicka Akim D. Macam. I am 20 years old and am a passionate IT student based in Valenzuela City, pursuing my Bachelor Degree in Information Technology at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela. My journey in technology is driven by the belief that software shouldn't just function correctly behind the scenes—it should also deliver an exceptional, intuitive experience for the user.

Over the course of my academic career, I realized that while I may not be a super programmer focused solely on writing complex code like other students, my true strength lies in ensuring that the software actually works and looks exceptional. This insight helped me discover my interest for Quality Assurance (QA) testing, User Interface design, and project documentation. Whether I am meticulously mapping out student test cases, managing timeline spreadsheets, or auditing interfaces for visual flaws, I thrive on strict organization, cross-functional communication, and thorough execution.
